Urban Farm Fights Chronic Illness East of Anacostia River

By Brittany McAlister / July 15, 2026

A sign inside THEARC Farm reads, “THEARC Farm straight ahead!” The photo was taken in Southeast Washington, D.C., on July 14, 2026. (Brittany McAlister/Georgetown University)

WASHINGTON, D.C. — A shortage of volunteers at a Southeast Washington urban farm threatens a key health initiative. Building Bridges Across the River operates a free market to combat food insecurity and health issues, but currently relies on limited staffing.

Data indicates low access to fresh food in the area, leading to health disparities. The farm relies on community labor for its "Food and Farm Fridays" program, supported by partnerships like the Capital Area Food Bank.

J.J. Boone, THEARC Farm’s deputy director, smiles for a photo in Southeast Washington, D.C., on July 14, 2026. (Brittany McAlister/Georgetown University)

As food access challenges persist following local store closures, efforts continue to support local nutrition.
